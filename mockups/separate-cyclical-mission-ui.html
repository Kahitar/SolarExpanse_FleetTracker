Separate Cyclical Mission UI: three FleetTracker tab options

All three concepts keep Cyclical missions as their own tab inside the existing FleetTracker panel rather than mixing them into transit/planned mission rows. Mock data is representative of the fields available from cyclical mission objects.

Option 1: Mission CardsBest default: stacked route cards optimized for the current FleetTracker style.
Option 2: Operations MatrixBest for comparing many routes quickly with compact columns and filters.
Option 3: Schedule TimelineBest for understanding what happens next and spotting operational problems.

Pros

  • Most readable at FleetTracker panel size.
  • Matches the existing grouped-row FleetTracker pattern.
  • Easy to make read-only first, then add safe native actions.

Cons

  • Less dense than a table when many routes exist.
  • Requires scrolling for large empires.
  • Side detail area may need to collapse on small resolutions.

Pros

  • Clarifies what will happen next instead of only listing definitions.

  • Complements planned/transit mission tabs well.

Cons

  • Depends on reliable next-event timing data; may need fallback estimates.

  • Can obscure the full route list if many events belong to one route.
